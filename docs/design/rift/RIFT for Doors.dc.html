CONTEXT READ
Built from POCKETOS-DS-v0.1.md, themes.json and pos_styles.c: IBM Plex Sans 16/20/24 + Mono 14/16/20, 56 px status bar with four cells, 72 px header with 72×56 back slab, hairline panels (radius 6, caption in the rule), slabs on surface, 2 px focus outline for pressed/selected, no shadows/gradients/blur. Every colour below is a token; nothing is hard-coded orange.
RIFT ACCENT = radio_rx (alias)
RIFT declares one token alias in its manifest (DS §8 permits this): rift_link → radio_rx. It marks what is heard directly and RX activity. Relayed traffic uses text_secondary, unknown uses text_muted with a literal ?. Selection, focus and primary actions stay on accent_primary, so RIFT never competes with the system accent and re-themes with Doors (proof strip at the bottom).
NAVIGATION
Four sections in a 40 px caption strip under the standard Doors header: ACTIVITY · NODES · COMMS · NET (active = accent_primary + 2 px underline, the focus model). Messages and channels merge into COMMS as one list; a channel is a row like a contact. A 48 px command line pinned at the bottom (kept from old RIFT) is the fifth navigator: /nodes, /msg hytta, /path rpt-7, and free text sends to the open conversation. Total fixed chrome: 56 + 72 + 40 + 48 = 216 px, leaving 1016 px for data.
DEVIATION TO APPROVE · RIFT-DEV-1
List rows are 36 px (DS §7 says 64). Conditions mirror DEV-1: a row tap only selects (never sends or deletes); the selected row expands to a 56 px action bar with full-size 56 px buttons; the hit area is the full row width. Paths longer than 3 hops: the hop strip shows the first two relays, a count, and the last relay; the numeric hop count is always printed next to it, so nothing is lost when the strip compresses.
