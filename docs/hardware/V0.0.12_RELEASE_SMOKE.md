Observations, not failures:

- **The unit's address changed from `192.168.10.157` to `192.168.10.171`.**
  After the flash, `eth0` (the USB Ethernet adapter, whose MAC is random on
  every boot) received `.157`. It had carrier but passed no traffic: the unit
  could not ping its gateway. Wi-Fi came up at `.171` once the store was
  restored, and SSH works there. It looks like the bench network (a lease
  bound to something other than the MAC), not the image. It is recorded for
  whoever next scripts against `.157`.
- The factory first boot had no IPv4 on `eth0` when the console ran its
  checks (`udhcpc: no lease, forking to background`); a lease arrived later.

Not repeated on the flashed card: RF. The deploy gate below, which ran the same
build, had a direct message ACKed by the T-Deck. Messages from the peer and a
channel message confirmed on the peer need the owner at the T-Deck.
