# Doors 0.4.0 candidate: fresh-card hardware test

**Build under test: `0.4.0`, `BUILD_ID=21baeef0`** (release/v0.4.0 commit
`21baeef0`), the candidate image itself, on unit A with its own card (Doors
0.3.9, `c03795ce`) taken out and kept unchanged. 2026-11-15.

Image: `sysimage-sdcard.img`, 763,363,328 bytes, sha256
`96612f252a68f9f85e9329a893174f99ff80f36da51b48b6763d07b66ad19846`, built
from a clean SDK output (docs/releases/v0.4.0.md, "Candidate image").

**No radio transmission was authorised or configured.** Automatic checks
ran over the serial console; the checks at the unit were made by the owner
and are marked as such.

## 1. Before: identity, compatibility, backup, recovery

- **Unit:** wlan0 `88:3b:dc:b7:9e:c7` (unit A), CH342 console on COM5.
  Device tree "Canaan CanMV-K230 with RM69A10 OLED", the panel the image's
  `k230_canmv_t_display_rm69a10_defconfig` is built for.
- **Backup:** `/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default`, `/etc/pocketos` and the
  release file of unit A's own card, sha256 `e5b3a772...591d` on the unit
  and on the PC (`C:\K230-artifacts\release-0.4.0\unitA-backup\`).
- **Recovery:** the test card is a separate card; unit A's own card is not
  written. Putting it back restores 0.3.9 as it was.

## 2. Flash

- **Target:** disk 1, "Mass Storage Device", USB, reader serial
  `121220160204`, 15,634,268,160 bytes, not boot or system (the spare reader
  of v0.3.6-v0.3.9). The owner approved the write.
- **Tool:** `flash-card.ps1` (disk number, serial, USB, size, not
  boot/system; source hash gate; unbuffered read-back). Dry run PASS, then
  an elevated run approved at UAC.
- **Result:** source gate PASS; 763,363,328 bytes in 59.9 s; **read back
  identical, `RESULT: FLASH PASS`** (log
  `C:\k230\out\doors-0.4.0-21baeef0\flash-card-20261115-190707.log`). The
  card was ejected through the shell.

## 4. Checks at the unit (owner)

- Cold boot from power off shows the Doors lock screen; swipe up opens the
  launcher - **PASS**.
- A tap opens an app; a short BOOT press goes back - **PASS**.
- The power key - **PASS**.
- Relay Nine: the picture in portrait and landscape, the corners, and the
  touch controls - **PASS**.
- Power off from Doors: the panel went dark, and it powered on normally
  again - **PASS**.
- RotorTerror's touch play was asked about but had no answer of its own;
  it is not recorded as checked.

**Fresh-card test PASSED** on build `21baeef0` (2026-11-15). No radio was
used.

**Not run:** RIFT, Wi-Fi, HDMI, a physical keyboard base, LINE TRACE and
COLOR, DeskBuddy presence with a person, Relay Nine at the Small and Large
text sizes, GENERAL 80 / GENERAL 365 / SEGMENT / INSTANCES (their files
are not part of Doors).
