# PacketShark NEURAL: G0, visibility honesty — frozen acceptance criteria

**Criteria set by the owner on 27 November 2026 and frozen in pull request #196, before any held-out validation.** Changing a criterion, a definition or one of the engine's decision values after a held-out result has been seen invalidates that held-out run. This page replaces G0's earlier wording in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) §2 ("≥ 99 % of affected intervals produce no PROBLEM claim", with a recall-under-loss threshold still to be set). That wording could be passed by an engine that always abstains.

G0 protects the brief's first question: is a reply slow, or is it simply not visible from here?

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem claim** | A WORSE verdict on any of the five comparisons of a mark ([N1.1.md](N1.1.md) §4): DNS answer time, DNS questions answered, DNS failures, DHCP DISCOVER rate, ARP request rate. |
| **False problem claim** | A problem claim on a metric whose true rate did not change in the harmful direction. A claim of a real benign rise (for example more ARP requests) is not false. |
| **Healthy window** | A comparison window in which no fault was injected. |
| **Healthy visibility-artifact categories** | Each healthy window is classed by what limited the capture's view of it (it may fall in two): **clean** (no artifact, counters read); **uniform loss** (0.1–60 % of frames lost, independent of kind); **burst loss** (time-correlated: drops in busy seconds of every kind, when answers are also slow); **HOLD**; **one-sided visibility** (this device sends, nothing reaches it); **counters failed** (a spell of failed lost-frame readings over the window). |
| **Material fault** | One whose effect exceeds the changes the engine treats as material: the resolver stops answering (≥ 70 % of answers missing), the resolver slows ≥ 3 times, SERVFAIL on ≥ 30 % of answers, ARP requests ≥ 5 times, DHCP DISCOVERs +0.3 a second or more. A subtle slowdown of 1.3–1.8 times is **not** material and is reported apart. |
| **Overlapping capture loss** | A fault in a window that also has uniform or burst capture loss. |

## 2. The criteria

| # | Criterion | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **G0-1** | False problem claims in each healthy visibility-artifact category: **at most 1 %** of windows | The one-sided 95 % Wilson upper bound is ≤ 1 % in every category |
| **G0-2** | Recall for material faults overlapping capture loss: **at least 50 %** | The one-sided 95 % Wilson lower bound is ≥ 50 % |
| **G0-3** | Reported separately, with no threshold: abstention (CAN'T TELL) on material faults; **false reassurance** (NO CHANGE on a faulted metric); per-fault recall, abstention and no-change; DNS recall by how often this device asks | Reported |
| **G0-4** | The replay fixtures ([N1.1.md](N1.1.md) §2) keep their expected ledgers: no problem claim in the healthy scenes (`one-sided`, `loss-healthy`, the rates in `loss-unknown`), the claim in the fault scenes, and every cited packet resolving or reading "packet expired" | `make packetshark-intel-test` passes |

## 3. What is frozen

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Engine and decision values | `core/pocketpcap/ppc_intel.[ch]` at the commit that adds this page. The provisional design values are retained (owner, 27 November 2026): alpha 0.01; minimum counts 5 pairs, questions or answers and 10 watched seconds; material changes (answered share halving, failures +25 points, rates ×3 or +6 a minute); answer time 1.5× and +20 ms; silent spell 1/1000 |
| Evaluation code | `research/packetshark-neural/e3a-r/e3a_r.py`: `g0()`, `g0_verdict()`, the categories and the material faults, at the same commit |
| Development set | E3a-R seeds 1000–1009 (10 × 40 sites × 60 windows). The engine's rules were tuned on these windows, so they are **not validation** |

## 4. Held-out validation (not run)

1. **Synthetic held-out.** E3a-R with seeds 2000–2009, never run before, with the generator, the engine and the evaluation unchanged. Run once and record the result as it comes out. No rule may be changed in response; a change means a new freeze and new seeds.
2. **An independent generator.** A reviewer who has not seen the engine's code writes a second generator from §1's definitions alone (Astra or Fabel review, HANDOVER §3 C). It is run once against the frozen engine.
3. **Real captures.** Replays of consented bench captures (the G3/G6 corpus), when it exists. This is the only validation that can speak about field behaviour.

G0 passes when 1 and 2 pass. Step 3 is required before any claim about real networks.

**Reading.**

- The burst-loss category, at an upper bound of 0.90 %, is the nearest to its limit. It is the one to watch in the held-out run.
- Recall under loss is about 3 points below the naive rule's (68.4 %). The naive rule pays for those points with 7 to 900 times more false claims in the artifact categories. The loss-aware rule's recall is 14 points above the binary gate's.
- Most misses are abstentions, and most abstentions come from too few of this device's own DNS questions. The own-traffic measurement on Unit A ([N1.1.md](N1.1.md) §10) shows how often that is the case on a real unit.
