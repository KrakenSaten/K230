# Doors 0.3.5 release notes

**Release candidate, not yet released.** Branch `release/v0.3.5`; no tag
and no published image yet. The candidate image, its checksums and the
fresh-card hardware test are recorded below once done.

Doors 0.3.5 follows v0.3.0 (tag `v0.3.0`, 2026-10-02). It is everything
merged to master up to `30c5d76` (PR #64), plus three release changes:
Doors' own code is Apache-2.0, the Vision detector model is no longer in the
image, and Vision runs without it. The BOOT-button navigation (PR #65,
merged to master after `30c5d76`) is **not** in this release.

  `nonai2d`, `vvcam`), B8 (the proprietary `rtl8723ds-bt` firmware, and
  firmware terms for `aic8800`), B9 (toolchain runtime licences and source
  offer not collected). `POCKETOS_REDISTRIBUTE` stays `NO`.

## Highlights

- **A freshly flashed card boots straight into Doors** (PR #46), and the
  LILYGO vendor launcher is no longer in the image (PR #47).
- **RIFT**: full-colour emoji and an emoji picker, Norwegian letters from
  the keyboard base (hold A or O), SYSTEM, MAP and NET views, message sounds
  and channel mute, repeater control (0-hop scan, login, status, commands),
  an RX LOG of every reception, late ACKs, RESEND, REPLY and CONTACTS, room
  for 1000 nodes, and the session kept in the background.
- **Storage**: USB drives (FAT32) in Files with safe eject, and growing the
  root filesystem over the whole microSD card from System.
- **Settings and System**: Settings in categories, System in tabs, Power &
  Sleep, time zone (DS §52).
- **Physical power key**: short press turns the screen off and on, a 1 s
  hold opens the power menu.
- **Zabbix**: server and sign-in set on the device (CONNECTION screen).
- **Vision without a detector model** (below).

## Vision

The detector model `yolov8n.kmodel` (the pinned SDK's compiled Ultralytics
YOLOv8n, AGPL-3.0), which images 0.2.1 to 0.3.0 carried for internal use, is
**not in this image**. A DOORS-trained replacement is in preparation.

- **Works**: COLOR, EDGE and LINE TRACE, which need no model. READ, FACE
  and RECOGNIZE still appear only when their models are installed by hand
  (none ships).
- **Off**: DETECT, TRACK and TRAFFIC. They are not offered, and Vision says
  under the picture: "No detector model on this unit: DETECT, TRACK and
  TRAFFIC are off". Vision opens in COLOR.
- **DeskBuddy** cannot see without a detector or a face model; it shows "NO
  VISION YET" and its clock and night mode work as before.
- The build refuses any of the SDK's Ultralytics YOLO kmodels anywhere in
  the image (by name and by hash) and the vendor `yolo` demo package.

## RIFT on a fresh card

Facts from the code (S60radiod, S65meshcored, Controls), not yet run end to
end from a freshly flashed card:

1. **Radio activation and meshcored are two separate switches**, and
   neither is on in a fresh image. radiod runs the **mock** backend until
   `/etc/default/radiod` says otherwise; meshcored, the MeshCore service RIFT
   talks to, is disabled until `/etc/default/meshcored` says otherwise.
2. **Controls' LoRa radio tile does not choose the backend.** It switches
   the radio on or off (with the antenna prompt when switching on) on
   whichever backend radiod runs. On the mock it reads "Receiving" and has
   nothing to do with the SX1262.
3. **A shell is required once**, and the on-device **Terminal** is enough
   (SSH needs a root password set first). On a unit with the antenna on
   MMCX1:

   ```sh
   echo RADIOD_BACKEND=sx1262 > /etc/default/radiod
   echo MESHCORED_ENABLE=1 > /etc/default/meshcored
   /etc/init.d/S60radiod restart
   /etc/init.d/S65meshcored restart
   ```

   Then switch the radio on in Controls (LoRa radio, "Enable radio" after
   the antenna prompt) and open RIFT. Avoid toggling the tile while radiod
   is still on the mock: switching it off and on again stores "on", and the
   real radio then starts on without the prompt.

Nothing in the UI writes either file today; a first-use flow in Settings or
RIFT would be its own change.

  what is under it.

- Wave retries a history save that failed.

## Upgrading from v0.3.0

A flash replaces the whole card, including the SSH host key and meshcored's
identity: save `/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and `/etc/pocketos` first
and put them back after the first boot. A unit that relied on the image's
detector model loses DETECT, TRACK and TRAFFIC.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.3.0 image (tag `v0.3.0`) and restore the same three
directories.

## Known limitations

- DETECT, TRACK and TRAFFIC need a detector model this image does not ship.
- RIFT needs one shell session on a fresh card (above).
- Repeated Camera or Vision open/close can still freeze the unit (the known
  camera lock-up).
- Everything listed for v0.3.0 that is not fixed above still applies
  (docs/releases/v0.3.0.md, docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
