## New since v0.3.7

### Relay Nine (PR #104)

- **A new game, Games > Relay Nine**: a short first-person level in an
  original industrial setting. Restore the upper relay, reach the exit; nine
  enemies, the carbine and the BREACH-2 shotgun, ammunition and medical
  pickups, an optional maintenance route, pause, help, death and victory.
  No save; Back/Home or a rotation abandons the run. All art and sound are
  original first-party recipes (docs/apps/RELAY_NINE.md,
  RELAY_NINE_POLISH.md).
- Touch pads, or the keyboard base (W/S, A/D, arrows, Space, F, R, Q, P/Esc).
- **Sound effects** play through a new helper, `/usr/bin/pos-audio-effects`,
  started while the game is open and stopped when it closes; it follows the
  system volume and mute.
- Ran on unit B's panel with a hand-installed shell and helper on
  2026-10-22, about 30 fps median (docs/apps/RELAY_NINE_DEVICE.md).

### RIFT

- **Explicit routes for a direct message** (PR #100, #102). meshcored can
  send one message by a route the user chose (`mesh.path_preview`,
  `mesh.send` with `via`; docs/api/mesh.md) and never falls back to AUTO for
  it. In RIFT, **SET ROUTE** in a direct conversation opens MAP as a route
  builder: pick repeaters in order, every edit is checked by the service
  without transmitting, and **USE ROUTE** / **USE DIRECT** stage that exact
  route for the next SEND; **AUTO** returns to the service's choice. The
  lines on the map are a requested route, not radio links or delivery.
- **Sentence capitals and a larger emoji picker** (PR #105): at the start
  of a message and after `.`, `!` or `?`, the touch keyboard offers a
  capital through its own one-shot SHIFT (one tap on SHIFT declines it; the
  keyboard base types what its own Shift says). The emoji picker is larger,
  its 18 px artwork drawn at whole multiples.

### DeskBuddy

- **Camera presence in BUDDY** (PR #103). DeskBuddy now opens the shipped
  R0 detector (before, it asked for `yolov8n.kmodel`, which no image has
  carried since 0.3.5, so it could not see). A new switch,
  **Camera presence · Buddy**, on by default: BUDDY wakes when somebody
  arrives and settles after they leave, and says CAMERA UNAVAILABLE when it
  cannot see. Presence is not identity: R0 tells a person, not who.
- **Visit sound · Guard** (PR #106, ADR-010 Amendment 2), **off by
  default**: an armed guard plays one short chime for each new visit it
  logs, through `pos-record` at the system volume. The chime is made by
  DeskBuddy at run time (`visit.wav` in its runtime directory); no sound file
  is shipped.

### Neon theme (PR #98)

- **Settings > Appearance > Neon**, an optional system-wide theme in the
  owner-selected Arcade direction. Doors stays the default and every
  existing theme keeps its look.

### Smaller changes

- Help & Feedback's QR colours are named through the design system
  (PR #95, merged after the 0.3.7 image was built); the code still draws
  black on white.
- `pos-browser`'s tile fetcher is rebuilt when the build identity changes,
  so its User-Agent names the right version (PR #101).
- Test-only: the first-boot test reaps its stand-in under a private PID
  namespace (PR #94).

## Known limitations

- **Relay Nine:** no physical keyboard has been used with it on hardware
  (Q, rollover and key hardware untested; the keyboard path was exercised
  by injection only), and the BREACH-2 shotgun's pickup, switching, firing
  and reload were not exercised on the device. p95 frame time on unit B is
  about 45-51 ms.
- **DeskBuddy:** in both visit-sound runs on unit B, R0 reported a person at
  the desk in every report while the owner said they had left the view;
  earlier the same day it reported the desk empty. Whether something at the
  desk is detected persistently or the owner stayed in view is not known,
  and a second visit after a departure was shown on the host only. Presence
  is not identity.
- **RIFT routing:** a route chosen in RIFT has not been carried by a
  physical repeater; the only radio result is one zero-relay direct message
  from unit B to a MeshCore companion (PR #100). The UI's own send and the
  device re-check of the builder's last two fixes are unverified on
  hardware. The map's lines are a requested route; nothing proves a chosen
  repeater can hear the previous one, and the collision check is fail-closed
  best effort (docs/validation/RIFT_EXPLICIT_PATH_P0.md). Physical keyboard
  typing gets no automatic capital, by design.
- **Build:** reproducing the image from the tag on a fresh build host needs
  `fix/fresh-build-fortran-toolchain` merged ("Release image").
- Everything listed for v0.3.7 still applies (docs/releases/v0.3.7.md,
  docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md): HDMI chosen at boot only, no battery level, R0 is a
  beta, stopping a camera stream can freeze the unit, Poker deals a fresh
  table on a rotation.
