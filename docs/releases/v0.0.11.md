# Doors 0.0.11 release notes (draft)

**Draft, not released.** VERSION is 0.0.11. No release candidate image has
been built, nothing has been flashed, and the release gate on unit A has not
been run; "Before this can be released" at the end lists what is owed. Until
then everything here that reached the device did so on a bench unit updated
with `deploy.sh`, not from a v0.0.11 image.

Doors 0.0.11 is the second Doors release for the LILYGO T-Display K230,
following v0.0.10 (tag `v0.0.10`, 2026-09-16).

Doors' own licence is not decided. No licence is granted and redistribution of
Doors, as source or as an image, is not authorised (docs/LICENSING.md).

Evidence words, as everywhere in this repository: **device** means seen on
unit A, **RF** means on air against another radio, **host** means the test
suites on the PC (and the simulator) only.

## What changed since v0.0.10

### The DOORS environment

- **Lock screen** at every cold start: the lock photograph, the time and
  date, "Swipe up to open" (Enter, Space and Up open it too). It is not
  security - there is no code and services keep running; it keeps a pocket
  from opening apps. `lock_screen=0` in settings.conf starts open. An alarm
  can be stopped while locked. *Device (DS §31 gate, 2026-09-23).*
- **A rotation no longer unlocks the device.** Turning the display restarts
  the shell in place; it now comes back exactly as locked or open as it was,
  including when the keyboard base is attached or removed in Automatic.
  *Host (2026-09-23); not yet seen on the device.*
- **Grouped launcher**: Connections, Workspace, Play and Device panels on the
  home photograph, every app one tap away, with Lock and Controls at the
  foot. *Device.*
- **Controls**: Radio, Wi-Fi, Rotation, Display mode and Brightness, rows to
  Settings, RIFT and System, and Lock and Power (Power opens System, whose
  actions confirm). *Device.*
- **The Doors theme** (sand accent on warm dark) is the default for a fresh
  card; the earlier themes stay in Settings > Appearance. Active choices in
  Settings and System use an accent chip instead of the radio's RX colour.
  *Host; DS §32 is PROPOSED until its unit A visual gate.*

### Landscape

- **Every app that is having a landscape layout has one**: Calculator,
  Notes, Settings, System, Clock, Calendar, Fleet and Radar, each with its
  own accepted design amendment (DS §22-§29) and a unit A gate. *Device, on
  bench builds of their day.* Timber stays portrait on purpose; Radio and
  Wave stretch across the landscape body.
- **A compact status bar in landscape apps** (32 px instead of 56 px) gives
  the body the height; the launcher and portrait keep the full bar, and Fleet
  keeps it too. The radio chip no longer clips "RX" in the compact bar.
  *Host; DS §30 is PROPOSED and its unit A gate has not been run.*
- **Clock keeps a running stopwatch, countdown and snooze across a
  rotation.** *Device (2026-09-18).*

### Mesh: MeshCore and RIFT

- **RIFT**, a new app: the mesh client. ACTIVITY (the radio service's state,
  this node, the ADVERT buttons, recent nodes, the raw frame feed), NODES
  (every node heard, path, signal, MESSAGE / RE-ROUTE / FORGET) and COMMS
  (direct conversations and channels, each with a composer). RIFT transmits
  only when a button is pressed. *RF (unit A against a T-Deck RIFT peer,
  2026-09-19 to 09-22).*
- **meshcored**, the MeshCore protocol service, on top of radiod. It is in
  the image and **off by default** (below). Up to 256 nodes are retained.
  *RF.*
- **radiod** gains an asynchronous transmit (`radio.send_async`) and a radio
  lease, so one client owns the radio at a time. *RF (through meshcored).*
- **Channel conversations belong to the channel, not its slot.** A channel
  left and a different one added in its place no longer inherits the old
  channel's history, and a reply from the old conversation is refused
  instead of going to the new channel. *Host (2026-09-23).*

### Also

- **Wi-Fi:** a wpa_supplicant answer that arrives after netd stopped waiting
  can no longer be taken for the answer to the next command, which could
  drop a working DHCP lease for a few seconds. *Host (2026-09-23).*
- **Build provenance:** the apply manifest and BUILD_INFO report the Doors
  tree's own clean or dirty state (they reported the Crypto checkout's).
- The third-party notices name the shell by its v0.0.10 path,
  `/usr/bin/doors-shell`.

## For operators

- **Commands.** `doors version` prints `Doors 0.0.11 (build <id>)`; `pos`
  stays an alias.
- **Image.** `doors-0.0.11-tdisplay-k230-<build_id>.img.gz` with its
  `.sha256`, as in v0.0.10.
- **meshcored is off until a unit is set up for it.** It needs radiod on the
  SX1262 backend, the vendor launcher off, and the antenna on MMCX1
  confirmed before any transmit (docs/services/MESHCORED.md):

  ```sh
  echo RADIOD_BACKEND=sx1262 >> /etc/default/radiod
  echo MESHCORED_ENABLE=1 > /etc/default/meshcored
  /etc/init.d/S60radiod restart && /etc/init.d/S65meshcored restart
  ```

  Enabled, the node takes the radio, listens on 869.618 MHz at 2 dBm by
  default and answers messages addressed to it.
- **Per-unit files are the unit's.** `/etc/default/*`, settings.conf, the
  Wi-Fi store and meshcored's identity under `/var/lib/pocketos` are not
  written by `deploy.sh`; a flash replaces them all.

## Upgrading from v0.0.10

- **Recommended: flash the image.** As in v0.0.10, a flash replaces the whole
  card: notes, saves, Wi-Fi networks, logs, unit settings, the SSH host key
  and `/root/.ssh/authorized_keys` are new. **A flash also gives the unit a
  new MeshCore identity**: meshcored creates a new key on its first enabled
  start, so peers see a new node. To keep the identity, save
  `/var/lib/pocketos/meshcored/` before flashing and put it back, with its
  0700/0600 modes, before enabling meshcored.
- First boot still shows the vendor launcher; hand the panel to Doors as in
  v0.0.10 (`/etc/default/k230_phone_ui` ENABLE=0, `/etc/default/doors-shell`
  ENABLE=1, reboot).
- A bench update with `deploy.sh` keeps `/var/lib/pocketos` and every
  `/etc/default` file, and does not change the boot partition.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.0.10 image (tag `v0.0.10`). The meshcored state directory is
ignored by v0.0.10, which has no meshcored.

## Known limitations

- **Landscape corners.** The image ships the provisional 30 px corner
  squares. Unit A needed 50 px top corners in landscape; whether that becomes
  the default is an owner decision still pending.
- **The boot splash does not always appear** (vendor bootloader display
  start-up, unchanged since v0.0.10).
- **Keyboard base:** attach and remove it only with the board powered off
  and USB power removed.
- **No real-time clock; time is UTC.** Until NTP answers the date is 1970:
  Notes then shows 1970 dates and orders such notes unreliably, and an advert
  sent then is ignored by peers that already know this node - advert after
  the clock is set.
- **A message's state says whether it was accepted, not whether it was
  transmitted.** A channel message reads `SENT · FLOOD` whether or not it went
  out, and nothing ever confirms a channel message: channels are an
  unacknowledged flood. Only a direct message's DELIVERED (its ACK) confirms
  anything.
- **RIFT lists the 64 most recently heard nodes** of the up to 256 meshcored
  holds; the rest are counted.
- Wi-Fi, Ethernet and Wave limits are as in v0.0.10.

The full list is docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md ("Open for v0.0.11" first).

## Before this can be released

Owed, in order; none of it is done:

1. Owner decisions: the landscape corner default (30 or 50 px), and whether
   DS §30 and DS §32 are accepted on the strength of the gate below.
2. A release candidate image built once from a clean clone of the frozen
   commit, with a clean (not `-dirty`) build id, and its checksums recorded.
3. The unit A gate on that image, flashed fresh: identity and defaults on
   first boot; the §30.7 and §32 visual checks in both orientations; the lock
   across rotation and keyboard-base changes; app smoke with persistence
   across reboot and power-off; meshcored and RIFT on air against a peer
   (adverts, direct messages, channels, a channel left and re-added); service
   recovery after a crash; Wi-Fi join and DHCP.
4. The smoke record (`docs/hardware/V0.0.11_RELEASE_SMOKE.md`), then the tag.
