# Doors 0.0.12 release notes

**Draft, in preparation (2026-09-24).** Not released, not tagged. RC1 is
built from `a8b1a9f`: `doors-0.0.12-rc1-tdisplay-k230-a8b1a9f.img.gz`,
sha256 `277012554db2f48b99dcfcd34311df589c7494de882d30fde79bb4540c65ac75`.
Its userspace passed a gate on unit A as a deploy
(docs/hardware/V0.0.12_RELEASE_SMOKE.md). The image itself has not yet been
flashed and booted; that and the owner's decision are what stand between RC1
and the tag.

Doors 0.0.12 is the third Doors release for the LILYGO T-Display K230,
following v0.0.11 (tag `v0.0.11`, 2026-09-23).

Doors' own licence is not decided. No licence is granted and redistribution of
Doors, as source or as an image, is not authorised (docs/LICENSING.md).

Evidence words, as everywhere in this repository: **device** means seen on
unit A, **RF** means on air against another radio, **host** means the test
suites on the PC (and the simulator) only.

## What changed since v0.0.11

### Files

- **Files**, a new app in the Device group: browse every folder, read text
  files (up to 32 KB), and create, rename, copy, move and delete, with a
  confirmation before any delete. The places Doors and the system depend on
  (`/etc`, `/root/.ssh`, `/var/lib/pocketos` and the like) are read-only: they
  can be opened and copied from, never changed. Copies and moves run in the
  background and can be left mid-way. A landscape layout with a details pane.
  *Device (unit A gate on `2bccc5b`, 2026-09-23, docs/hardware/FILES_GATE.md);
  DS §33 accepted.* Enter on the keyboard base was not run on the device (host
  only).
- **The landscape launcher** now holds 13 apps: the Device group wraps to a
  second line and the launcher scrolls to Lock and Controls. *Device;
  accepted with DS §33.*

### Fullscreen apps

- **RIFT, Notes, Wave, Fleet, Radar and Timber are fullscreen**: no status
  bar in either orientation, and their pages start at the top edge. Whatever
  they showed in the bar - Fleet's turn, Radar's and Timber's run state,
  Wave's MIC ON, a Notes storage error - is shown at the right end of their
  header instead. Every other screen keeps its bar as before. *Device (unit A
  gate on `a30678e`, 2026-09-24, docs/hardware/FULLSCREEN_APPS_GATE.md);
  DS §30.8 accepted.*
- **The lock over a fullscreen app** shows the status bar as it does over any
  other app, and it is gone again as soon as the app shows through on
  unlocking. *Device.*
- **Fleet in landscape**: the board's squares are taller (40 px, from 34) at
  the same width (51 px), so aiming is easier and the columns beside the
  board are unchanged. *Device.*
- **Timber in portrait**: the tower's table is taller (728 px, from 672), with
  the controls still at the foot. *Device.*
- **Notes**: the editor above the touch keyboard is 156 px tall in landscape
  (124 px before) and 56 px taller in portrait. *Device.*

### Also

- `shell.info` reports the status bar as drawn (`chrome.shown_height`) next
  to the chrome in force.
- A test of Wave's error path that could fail on a loaded build host now waits
  for the audio helper to exit; Wave itself is unchanged.

## For operators

- **Commands.** `doors version` prints `Doors 0.0.12 (build <id>)`; `pos`
  stays an alias.
- **Image.** `doors-0.0.12-tdisplay-k230-<build_id>.img.gz` with its
  `.sha256`, as before.
- **meshcored** is still off until a unit is set up for it, exactly as in
  v0.0.11 (docs/releases/v0.0.11.md, docs/services/MESHCORED.md).
- **Per-unit files are the unit's.** `/etc/default/*`, settings.conf, the
  Wi-Fi store and meshcored's identity under `/var/lib/pocketos` are not
  written by `deploy.sh`; a flash replaces them all.

## Upgrading from v0.0.11

- **Recommended: flash the image.** As before, a flash replaces the whole
  card: notes, saves, Wi-Fi networks, logs, unit settings, the SSH host key
  and `/root/.ssh/authorized_keys` are new, and **meshcored makes a new
  MeshCore identity** unless `/var/lib/pocketos/meshcored/` is saved before
  and put back (0700/0600) before meshcored is enabled.
- First boot still shows the vendor launcher; hand the panel to Doors as in
  v0.0.10 (`/etc/default/k230_phone_ui` ENABLE=0, `/etc/default/doors-shell`
  ENABLE=1, reboot).
- A bench update with `deploy.sh` keeps `/var/lib/pocketos` and every
  `/etc/default` file.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.0.11 image (tag `v0.0.11`). Nothing v0.0.12 stores is read
differently by v0.0.11.

## Known limitations

As in v0.0.11 (docs/releases/v0.0.11.md), and:

- **Timber and Wave in landscape** are still their portrait pages in a wide
  body, which scroll (Wave's TRANSMIT is below the fold on the send page).
- **Radar in portrait** keeps its scope, which the width limits, so the page
  ends well above the foot.

The full list is docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md ("Open for v0.0.12" first).
