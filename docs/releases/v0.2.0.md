# Doors 0.2.0 release notes

Released 2026-09-28 (tag `v0.2.0`). The image is the build of `568836e`:
`doors-0.2.0-tdisplay-k230-568836e.img.gz`, sha256
`34e1c9803b7aef421b9744f9b1b1b658ae9a43c27a5b1ed605ceea12ab555bd8`
(raw `sysimage-sdcard.img`, 763,363,328 bytes, sha256
`0916d9c0a893a9e700405519c0c4e2cedc433f8e8b024fa09c7099d1fe11af65`). The
commits after `568836e` are documentation only.

Doors 0.2.0 follows v0.1.0 (tag `v0.1.0`, 2026-09-26). It is everything
merged to master since then. Each part passed its own hardware gate before
it was merged, so this release had one short combined smoke on unit B
(below), not a new validation campaign.

Doors' own licence is not decided. No licence is granted and redistribution of
Doors, as source or as an image, is not authorised (docs/LICENSING.md).

## What changed since v0.1.0

- **Vision** (new, a prototype): DETECT, TRACK and COUNT on the K230's KPU
  with the vendor SDK's YOLOv8n model, in its own helper `pos-vision`
  (DS §38, PROPOSED; docs/apps/VISION.md, docs/hardware/VISION_GATE.md).
  **The model is not in the image** (docs/LICENSING.md, open item 10): without
  `/usr/share/doors/vision/yolov8n.kmodel`, put there by hand with
  `tools/vision/install-model.sh`, Vision says there is no model.
- **RIFT**: landscape COMMS as a console (no shell header, a narrow virtual
  conversation list, the thread with the width: 17 whole messages where
  there were 11), identity accents from the theme engine, a 20-minute
  traffic graph on ACTIVITY, and a cache of 1000 nodes / 256 conversations /
  512 messages (DS §37, ACCEPTED 2026-09-28 on its unit B gate;
  docs/hardware/RIFT_UI_DENSITY_GATE.md). Also the earlier RIFT density,
  scale and DM-notification policy work (docs/hardware/RIFT_UI_NEXT_GATE.md).
- **Browser** (new): a first-party reader for simple web pages (text,
  links, JPEG and PNG) over HTTP and HTTPS, with bookmarks and recent pages,
  its fetching in the `pos-browser` helper (ADR-009; docs/apps/BROWSER.md,
  docs/hardware/BROWSER_GATE.md).
- **Recorder** (new): WAV voice and field recording through `pos-record`
  (ADR-010; docs/apps/RECORDER.md, docs/hardware/RECORDER_GATE.md).
- **Camera gallery**: grid, photo view, slideshow and export
  (docs/hardware/CAMERA_GALLERY_GATE.md).
- **Wave**: one screen, presets, history and landscape
  (docs/hardware/WAVE_NEXT_GATE.md).
- **Compact status cluster** in the top right (DS §36;
  docs/hardware/COMPACT_STATUS_CLUSTER_GATE.md).
- **HDMI output** on the LT9611 as a boot-time alternative to the AMOLED
  (`pos-display-boot hdmi && reboot`), 1280x720@60 and 1920x1080@60. **This is
  the first Doors image with Doors-owned kernel patches** (0070-0073, ADR-011;
  docs/hardware/HDMI_OUTPUT.md, docs/hardware/HDMI_GATE.md).
- **Fixes**: the exif build-identity rebuild rule; `art_format_test`
  counts sixteen icons.

The launcher has 18 apps.

Not covered by the smoke: **the release image itself was not flashed**, so
its patched kernel did not run on unit B. That kernel (`Image`, sha256
`e5c4c7f7…`) is byte-identical to the round-6 kernel of the HDMI gate on
unit A, which ran on the AMOLED with the shell, touch, spidev and radio `rx`
(R6.1 PASS) as well as on HDMI (docs/hardware/HDMI_GATE.md). No RF
transmission, no Browser, Recorder, Wave or gallery check (each had its own
gate).

Image checks (read from the image's partitions): the image's own gate PASS
(every service present, whole and from build `568836e`); against v0.1.0's
image, 8 files added (`pos-vision`, `pos-browser`, `pos-record`,
`pos-drmtest`, `pos-display-boot` and three icons), none removed; no kmodel in
the image; partition 1's `/Image` is the patched kernel.

## Upgrading from v0.1.0

As for v0.1.0: a flash replaces the whole card, including the SSH host key
and meshcored's identity, so save `/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and
`/etc/pocketos` first and put them back. To use Vision, install the model
afterwards (`tools/vision/install-model.sh <unit ip>`).

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.1.0 image (tag `v0.1.0`).

## Known limitations

As in v0.1.0, and:

- **The camera lock-up comes sooner with Vision.** Repeated open and close of
  a camera app can freeze the whole unit (a power cycle recovers it); Camera
  alone reached it after 21-40 opens on unit A, Vision within about 5-20
  stops on unit B. Accepted by the owner as the known vendor camera fault
  (docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md, "Vision"; docs/hardware/VISION_GATE.md).
- **The Vision model is not shipped** (licence unstated, docs/LICENSING.md
  item 10), and Vision's DS §38 is still PROPOSED.
- RIFT: conversation names truncate at about eight characters in the
  landscape list; a full list of 256 drops the oldest conversations and the
  channels; in portrait ACTIVITY the graph's caption runs into its legend
  until something has been heard.
- meshcored still holds 256 nodes; RIFT's 1000 is host-tested only.
- HDMI: no hotplug to userspace, and the HDMI device tree drops LoRa, touch
  and uart1 (docs/hardware/HDMI_OUTPUT.md).

The full list is docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md.
