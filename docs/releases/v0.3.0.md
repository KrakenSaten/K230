# Doors 0.3.0 release notes

Released 2026-10-02 (tag `v0.3.0` -> `2956eff`). The image is the build of
the tagged commit: `doors-0.3.0-tdisplay-k230-2956eff.img.gz`, sha256
`0360f4bc2ec8b01d24e41dfd2021eef0b1aadf262fbc894148de5ecc418c6337`
(214,659,565 bytes; raw `sysimage-sdcard.img`, 763,363,328 bytes, sha256
`9f7e43f0620259c458783fe8c7d4d9aa3105af365ca6429cf29325d8a585b3c2`).
`/etc/doors-release` says `0.3.0` and `BUILD_ID=2956eff`. The commits after
`2956eff` are documentation only (this record).

Doors 0.3.0 follows v0.2.1 (tag `v0.2.1`, 2026-09-28). It is everything
merged to master since then. Each part passed its own gate before it was
merged; this release had one fresh-flash smoke on one unit (below).

Doors' own licence is not decided. No licence is granted and redistribution of
Doors, as source or as an image, is not authorised (docs/LICENSING.md). The
image contains the AGPL-3.0 Vision model and is for internal development and
testing only (docs/LICENSING.md item 10).

## Highlights

- **Terminal 0.2**: a real shell on the device, now with a session that
  stays open while you are elsewhere, three text sizes and a CLI toolbox.
- **New apps**: Photo, Video (MP4/H.264 on the hardware decoder), MP3,
  DeskBuddy, and the games Solitaire, Blackjack and 2048. The launcher
  has 26 apps (18 in v0.2.1).
- **A new launcher**: three favourites, then Apps, Utilities and Games
  folders (DS §47, accepted).
- **System text size**: Small, Medium or Large, applied at once (DS §46,
  accepted).
- **The keyboard base's own keys**: F-key shortcuts and status LEDs.
- **RIFT management**: channels, node search, a hop view and device
  settings on the unit itself.

## RIFT

- Channel messages show the sender before the text.
- NODES has a find bar and a ZERO-HOP filter; NET shows the mesh as hop
  rings.
- ACTIVITY > CHANNELS lists, leaves and adds channels (hashtag, private with
  a random key, or a shared key).
- THIS DEVICE renames the node and sets the path hash size (1, 2 or 3
  bytes).
- Common emoji are shown as text smileys; captions fit at every text size.

## Terminal

- **Persistent session**: leaving the Terminal keeps the shell and its
  jobs running; coming back reattaches. **CLOSE SESSION** (with a
  confirmation) ends it (DS §49, accepted).
- **Text sizing**: the grid follows the system text size - Small, Medium
  or Large (148x20 down to 98x14 characters in landscape).
- **CLI toolbox** in the image: htop, nano, jq, strace, iperf3 and tcpdump
  (curl was already there). No package manager.
- Opens from the launcher, F8 or the LILYGO key; history, Tab completion,
  Ctrl chords and 1000 lines of scrollback.

## System / UI

- **Launcher grouping**: three favourite slots (tap an empty one, or long
  press, to choose), then Terminal, RIFT, Browser, Settings and the
  folders Apps, Utilities and Games. System moved into Settings.
- **Accessibility**: system text size Small / Medium / Large under
  Settings > Appearance, plus stronger contrast for muted text and heavier
  launcher glyphs.
- **Hardware controls**: F1 Home, F2 Settings, F3/F4 keyboard light, F5/F6
  volume, F7 screenshot, F8 Terminal, F9 RIFT, F10/F11 brightness; the
  mic key opens Wave; LEDs for Caps, microphone in use and camera in use.
- **Back corner**: the whole top-left corner of an app header is the Back
  button.

## Apps

- **Photo**: browse, view and delete Camera's pictures from their own app.
- **Video**: a local MP4 player on the K230's video decoder.
- **MP3**: a local music player.
- **DeskBuddy**: a desk companion (Companion, Desk Guard, Night) that can
  use Vision's camera pipeline.
- **Games**: Solitaire, Blackjack and 2048.
- **Fleet**: chat during a two-player match and a clear match status in the
  header (your turn, waiting, connecting, opponent gone).
- **Vision**: a mode picker, TRAFFIC and tracking improvements; READ, FACE
  and RECOGNIZE appear only when their models are installed by hand.

## Fixes / Stability

- Settings never overwrites a settings file it could not read completely;
  Browser never replaces its bookmarks after a failed read.
- Fleet's multiplayer AUTO deploy no longer places the same fleet every
  match.
- Vision: no phantom counts after a view change, the scene is seen
  upright, and the KPU is released reliably when leaving.
- RIFT headers and captions no longer clip at Large text.
- Terminal: no stray cursor block on the first frame.

## Upgrading from v0.2.1

A flash replaces the whole card, including the SSH host key and meshcored's
identity: save `/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and `/etc/pocketos` first
and put them back after the first boot, as for v0.2.1.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.2.1 image (tag `v0.2.1`) and restore the same three
directories.

## Known limitations

- **A rotation ends an active Terminal session** (so does attaching or
  detaching the keyboard base in Automatic rotation, or a shell restart);
  the Terminal reopens with a new shell.
- **htop 3.3.0 aborts now and then** (about 1 run in 10 in its gate, also
  outside Doors); restart it.
- The Terminal is a VT100 subset: one session, no copy/paste, no
  alternate screen or colour, no Home/End/PgUp/PgDn keys.
- RIFT: only common emoji become smileys (others show as a box); every
  flood is unscoped; renaming as seen by a peer and 2-byte path hashes are
  not yet tested on air.
- The top two-way control on the keyboard base is not identified and does
  nothing.
- At Large text some landscape labels end in "..." (Photo, Camera gallery,
  the DeskBuddy launcher name).
- Fleet's lobby can show stale players, and a re-sort can move the
  selection to another player.
- Video: only MP4/H.264 tested, seeks take about a second, rotation ends
  playback. MP3: short gaps between tracks, no background play.
- Vision's READ, FACE and RECOGNIZE models are not shipped (licences not
  cleared). Repeated Camera or Vision open/close can still freeze the unit
  (the known camera lock-up).
- **Internal images only**: Doors' licence is undecided and the image
  carries the AGPL-3.0 Vision model.

The full list is docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md.
