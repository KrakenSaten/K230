## New since v0.3.8

### Vision (PR #112)

- **MODE > GENERAL > DETECT > CLASSES** lists what the detector in force can
  name: R0's 6 classes, or COCO's 80 by supercategory with GENERAL 80. The
  status gains "In view: person 2, cup 1, ...".
- The 80-class detector is called **GENERAL 80** (was "UPSTREAM"; stored
  choices carry over). **The GENERAL 80 file is not part of Doors or this
  image** (see "Models and licences").
- **TOOLS > COLOR** reads a square on the picture as a named colour (or
  `mixed: A nn% and B`) with a swatch, filtered over a few readings; tap
  moves the square, **MATCH** highlights that colour, **CLEAR** stops it.
- **TOOLS > LINE TRACE** follows one line from the bottom of the picture,
  shows LOST with the time and a warning as soon as it is gone, and picks it
  up again where it was last seen.
- Shell and `pos-vision` speak **protocol 3** and must be installed
  together (an image always does).

### DeskBuddy (PR #111)

- When camera frames or detections stop, BUDDY says the camera is
  unavailable instead of deciding nobody is there; only a fresh empty report
  means absence.
- A Guard visit survives a vision outage: recovery does not log a second
  visit or play a second chime. A real departure and return still do.
- Owner-aware Guard behaviour is **not** added (the handoff in
  docs/apps/deskbuddy/PRESENCE_OWNER_HANDOFF.md says what it would need).
  Issue #109 (a stationary box on unit B) is not diagnosed or closed.

### Relay Nine (PR #107, #113)

- Gameplay uses the whole screen: the shell header hides and the 320 x 200
  picture is scaled as large as it fits, letterboxed rather than stretched.
  Pause, help and the end screens bring the header back.
- Faster exact shading and refined industrial artwork, weapons and reload
  animations (PR #107).

### RIFT (PR #110)

- **MAP > TRAFFIC**: a local, read-only view of received MeshCore traffic
  (the RX LOG ring RIFT already keeps): packet list, detail and the observed
  path on the map. Nothing is transmitted or stored on disk for it.

## Upgrading from v0.3.8

As for every release, a flash replaces the whole card: save
`/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and `/etc/pocketos` first. A locally
installed GENERAL 80 file under `/usr/share/doors/vision/` is not on the
new card; it has to be copied again with `tools/vision/install-model.sh
--general` from a file the owner holds.
