Released 2026-09-26 (tag `v0.1.0`). The image is the build of `1368695`:
`doors-0.1.0-tdisplay-k230-1368695.img.gz`, sha256
`739e7927c2a8368a034b512a543106854fc69df5e13c2a7c5812234ee2e71565`
(raw `sysimage-sdcard.img`, 763,363,328 bytes, sha256
`e29c34d135530741a32cc9827178a894943784d52b206ac7eaa0fc0f4e22d727`). The

Doors 0.1.0 follows v0.0.12 (tag `v0.0.12`, 2026-09-25). It is everything
merged to master since then; each part passed its own unit A gate before it
was merged, so this release had a short integrated smoke only (below).

## What changed since v0.0.12

- **Camera**: live preview and JPEG stills through the K230's V4L2 camera,
  owned by a per-screen helper (ADR-006, DS §34; docs/apps/CAMERA.md,
  docs/hardware/CAMERA_GATE.md).
- **Device controls and Diagnostics**: radio on/off in Controls with the
  SX1262 **off by default** until the owner answers the antenna question,
  system volume with mute, Bluetooth and battery status, and System >
  Diagnostics (DS §31.5; docs/hardware/DEVICE_CONTROLS_GATE.md).
- **Zabbix**: a read-only viewer for an existing Zabbix server, in the
  launcher's CONNECTIONS group, its API client in the `pos-zabbix` helper
  (ADR-007, DS §35; docs/apps/ZABBIX.md, docs/hardware/ZABBIX_UNIT_A_GATE.md).
- **Fleet multiplayer**: two Doors devices play Fleet over the MeshCore mesh,
  with forfeit reachable during a match and headlined as one (ADR-008,
  Proposed; docs/hardware/FLEET_MULTIPLAYER_GATE.md).
- **Fixes**: `deploy.sh` over Wi-Fi can no longer strand the unit; a busy
  `wpa_supplicant` no longer costs the saved networks; a restarted shell's
  first touch lands where the finger is.

## Upgrading from v0.0.12

As for v0.0.12 (docs/releases/v0.0.12.md): a flash replaces the whole card,
including the SSH host key and meshcored's identity, so save
`/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and `/etc/pocketos` first and put them
back. On a fresh card the SX1262 stays off until it is enabled from Controls.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.0.12 image (tag `v0.0.12`).

## Known limitations

As in v0.0.12, and:

- **First boot after a flash can hang** in the vendor host-key generation
  (seen once on unit A, 2026-09-26); power-cycle once. Not reproduced.
- Fleet multiplayer's ADR-008 is still Proposed; the lobby draws a box for
  characters the font lacks in node names.

The full list is docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md.
