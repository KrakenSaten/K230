# Doors 0.3.6 release notes

**Status: release candidate. Not tagged, not published.** Branch
`release/v0.3.6`, from master `cc3b37e` (PR #76). The candidate image is the
build of **`967fe82`** (`0.3.6`, `BUILD_ID=967fe82`), which adds R0's
Apache-2.0 licence to NOTICE; its record is under "Candidate image" below.
The commits after `967fe82` are this record and other documentation. The
fresh-card smoke test passed on unit B with the earlier build `9ad64a1`,
whose files differ from `967fe82` only in NOTICE, the notices and build
stamps. No download link points at 0.3.6 until its assets are published.

Doors 0.3.6 follows v0.3.5 (tag `v0.3.5`, 2026-10-07). It is everything
merged to master since then, plus Vision's own detector, **R0 · Beta**, in
the image as an experimental beta.

## Vision: R0 · Beta

- **The image carries a detector again**: R0, a YOLOX-Tiny 416 the Doors
  project trained itself, from no pretrained weights, on licence-filtered
  COCO 2017 and Open Images V7 pictures. DETECT, TRACK and TRAFFIC work on a
  freshly flashed card.
- **Six classes**: car, truck, bus, motorcycle, bicycle and person. Nothing
  else is boxed.
- **An experimental beta.** R0 can miss vehicles, including cars in ordinary
  street scenes; small and distant vehicles are mostly not found, and cars
  and trucks are sometimes confused. It is not claimed to be better than the
  detectors Vision used before. Treat TRACK and TRAFFIC counts as
  indications.
- **Selected on a fresh installation** and named **R0 · Beta** on the
  detector button and in DETECT's numbers. With R0 the only detector on the
  unit, the button only names it: no detector that is not there is offered.
- **Models installed by hand still work.** A bench unit given the upstream
  YOLOX-Tiny file gets the detector A/B (PR #76) as before; READ, FACE and
  RECOGNIZE appear when their models are installed. A stored choice whose
  file has gone opens the detector that is there and says so.
- **COLOR, EDGE and LINE TRACE** are unchanged.
- **DeskBuddy is unchanged.** It opens Vision's helper with its own default
  model, `yolov8n.kmodel`, which no image ships, so R0 does not reach it: a
  fresh card without face models still reports NO VISION YET.
- Measured on unit A with the same file (bench A/B, 2026-10-09): KPU 55-68 ms
  per frame, about 12.5 fps (docs/hardware/VISION_MODEL_AB_GATE.md).

Details: docs/apps/VISION.md, "The model".

## Licence

- **Doors' own code stays Apache-2.0** (LICENSE, NOTICE, ADR-013).
- **R0's weights are licensed under Apache-2.0** too (owner decision,
  2026-10-09; NOTICE in the image, MODEL_LICENSES.md, "R0: the licence
  decision"). That covers the Doors project's own rights in the model; it
  does not settle anyone else's. What R0 was made from keeps its own terms:
  YOLOX's code (Apache-2.0), and the training data (COCO 2017 and Open Images
  V7 annotations, CC BY 4.0; Flickr pictures under CC BY 2.0 or "No known
  copyright restrictions"), credited in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt (`yolox`,
  `r0-training-data`). No training picture, annotation or checkpoint is in
  the image.
- **The per-image attribution list** is published beside this release as
  `ATTRIBUTION.tsv`: 7,742,997 bytes, sha256
  `2bc99bc8b2098a4f628fa8f3d0c08af5252a0d4cbdfb1eddaad9f9062e594c3e`,
  listed in the release's SHA256SUMS. COCO records no photographer names, so
  its 10,408 rows name the photo's Flickr file instead.
- The image carries no other detector: `yolov8n.kmodel` and the upstream
  YOLOX-Tiny are refused by name and hash at build time and by the image
  gate.
- The third-party questions recorded for v0.3.5
  (docs/licensing/APACHE_2_READINESS.md §14) are unchanged.
- The build keeps `POCKETOS_REDISTRIBUTE = NO`, so `make legal-info` still
  does not archive Doors' own package source; that source is this public
  repository at the release tag. v0.3.5's notes said this would change in
  the next build; it has not (notices_test still requires NO until the
  readiness audit clears it), which is the owner's call.

## Build and provenance

- The R0 kmodel is a build input kept outside git. `apply_to_sdk.sh` takes
  it from `POCKETOS_VISION_R0_KMODEL` and accepts it only with the sha256 in
  `tools/vision/r0-model.sha256`; the package checks the pin again when it
  installs it; the SDK's applied-source manifest, the provenance summary and BUILD_INFO
  record it; `verify_image.sh` refuses an image without exactly that file
  or with `yolov8n.kmodel` or the upstream YOLOX-Tiny.
- R0's own record: checkpoint `best_ckpt.pth` epoch 299 (`a501b84c…5d6f`),
  ONNX `48722fb6…ab9c`, kmodel `94a20ac0…d268` compiled with nncase 2.11.0
  (docs/vision/R0_TRAFFIC6_RESULTS.md on `research/yolox-traffic-training`,
  `c710dee`). It was verified by hash, not retrained or recompiled.

## The known camera lock-up

What the release notes have called "the known camera lock-up" since v0.1.0
(docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md, "Hardware and BSP" and "Vision"). Nothing about it
changed in 0.3.6; this section collects the evidence in one place.

- **What happens:** the whole unit stops - panel, touch, serial console and
  network - and only a power cycle recovers it. Nothing is printed at the
  moment it happens (console at loglevel 8: no oops, panic, RCU stall or
  watchdog); the last line is the normal `vvcam_isp_release` of the last
  camera close.
- **Exact trigger, as far as it is known:** stopping a camera stream,
  repeatedly. Every recorded freeze came a few seconds after a camera close
  (`vvcam_isp_release`), while the unit was idle or starting the next
  action, never while a picture was streaming. Stopping is what Camera,
  Vision, the Vision helper's `bench` and DeskBuddy's provider do when they
  are left or end. VERIFIED counts:
  - Camera alone, open - about 3 s of preview - leave, in a loop (unit A,
    2026-09-27, master binaries and v0.1.0's `pos-camera`): froze after 21,
    27 and 40 opens in three runs (docs/hardware/CAMERA_GALLERY_GATE.md §6).
  - Vision with a detector running on the KPU (unit B, 2026-09-28, the
    YOLOv8n model): froze within about 5-20 stops, three times in one day;
    streams stopped part-way froze it 2 times in 24, while the KPU alone,
    the camera stream alone and both run to completion never froze (30 runs
    each). On the same unit and boot Camera ran 40 cycles clean.
    Blocking signals in the helper did not cure it
    (docs/hardware/VISION_GATE.md).
  - Runs that did not freeze: Vision TRAFFIC 5 open/close cycles
    (docs/hardware/VISION_TRAFFIC_GATE.md); unit A on v0.3.5 with the
    detector A/B, 17 helper starts and stops, R0 among them
    (docs/hardware/VISION_MODEL_AB_GATE.md); the v0.3.6 smoke test below.
- **Cause:** not established. Suspected: the vendor's camera stack -
  `isp_media_server` (binary only) with the out-of-tree vvcam modules - the
  same class as the STREAMOFF+STREAMON lock-up Camera worked around in
  docs/hardware/CAMERA_GATE.md. Finding it needs kernel-side debugging or
  the vendor.
- **In v0.3.5:** yes, by the same code: the camera stack, `pocketcam`,
  `pos-camera` and `pos-vision` have no source change from v0.3.5 to 0.3.6,
  and v0.3.5's notes list it. It was not reproduced on a v0.3.5 image
  specifically. What 0.3.6 does change is exposure: on a fresh 0.3.5 card
  Vision has no detector, so it never runs the detector stream that reached
  the freeze soonest; on 0.3.6 DETECT is the default again, as it was on
  0.2.1 to 0.3.0.
- **Does it block ordinary use?** Not on the evidence. Opening Camera or
  Vision, using it and leaving a handful of times per boot stayed below
  every count at which it was seen. Someone who opens and closes Vision many
  times in one boot can reach it, and then has to power-cycle; no damage to
  stored data was seen after the recorded freezes, which is an observation,
  not a guarantee. It stays a known limitation, not fixed.

## Upgrading from v0.3.5

As for every release, a flash replaces the whole card: save
`/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default` and `/etc/pocketos` first and put them
back after the first boot.

- A restored Vision `settings.v1` without a `detector=` line selects R0. One
  that says `detector=upstream` on a card without the upstream file opens R0
  and says so.
- A `yolov8n.kmodel` installed by hand is still the helper's default
  (DeskBuddy, `pos-vision` without `--model`); Vision itself runs R0.

## Rolling back

Flash the v0.3.5 image (tag `v0.3.5`) and restore the same three
directories.

## Known limitations

- R0 is a beta: it can miss vehicles, including cars in street scenes, and
  small or distant ones mostly; it detects only its six classes.
- DeskBuddy does not use R0 (above).
- Repeated Camera or Vision open/close can still freeze the unit until it
  is power-cycled (the known camera lock-up, above). With R0, DETECT is the
  default again, so Vision runs the detector stream that reached it soonest.
- Poker deals a fresh table on a rotation.
- Everything listed for v0.3.5 that is not fixed above still applies
  (docs/releases/v0.3.5.md, docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
