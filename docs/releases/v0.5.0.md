## Highlights

### PacketShark, new in the image

A receive-only network analyzer under **Apps → PacketShark**, with its
capture helper `pos-capture` (ADR-016; docs/apps/PACKETSHARK.md):

- Capture on **Ethernet or Wi-Fi** (managed mode: the traffic the unit's own
  Wi-Fi link carries; no monitor mode). LIVE counters, a 60-second traffic
  graph stacked by protocol and the protocol mix (PRs #136-#141, #146).
- **PACKETS** with filter and sort, **INSPECT** layer by layer with hex,
  pins, **Signals** for DNS failures, resets, retransmissions, unreachables
  and unanswered SYNs, and **SAVE** of a stopped capture to a `.pcap` file.
- **DNS and TCP diagnosis** (M11.1, PR #149): queries paired with answers,
  response times, NXDOMAIN/SERVFAIL, handshakes, resets and retransmissions
  by kind.
- **Network intelligence** (M12.1, PR #185): the devices seen, DHCP
  exchanges, servers and leases, ARP pairs, conflicts and moves, and a list
  of findings each with its evidence, on a network overview reached from
  LIVE.
- It never transmits, injects or redirects anything. Fit fixes for Large
  text and quiet captures (PRs #148, #150, #184).

### Games

- **Games Hub** (PR #144, DS §60): **Games** opens a poster wall with
  RotorTerror and Relay Nine as featured posters and a poster for each of
  the other games, in portrait and landscape. Its Multiplayer Lobby poster
  is a preview only.
- **Lamplight card tables** (PRs #179, #180, #183): Blackjack, Poker and
  Solitaire get illustrated court cards, motion, a full-screen table and
  serif headlines (Fraunces, OFL-1.1), on one shared card, RNG and shoe
  module.

### RIFT Activity (PR #140)

ACTIVITY shows the MeshCore traffic the radio hears: a live signal scope,
20- or 5-minute stacked bars in packet-type colours, a per-type legend and a
live feed. Once RIFT has been opened, new messages raise a banner anywhere
in Doors. MeshCore radio operation stays off on a fresh card.

### DeskBuddy 2.0 (PR #151)

**Pip**, a touch-responsive owl on a perch: stroke to pet, hold to rest,
tap to wake, drag a biscuit to its beak. An optional character voice
(Settings: Off by default, Touch only, All).

### Zabbix (PR #152)

The Zabbix viewer is redesigned as a card-based monitoring console.

### DOORS Companion: unit side only

The source now holds the **DOORS Companion** Android app
(`android/doors-companion`) and its unit service **`gatewayd`** (ADR-019):
RIFT read and send (PRs #165-#170), a PacketShark instrument (PRs #173,
#175-#178, #181, #182) and a live Vision picture (PR #174, Vision M6).
The image carries the unit-side pieces that the shell and helpers need
(the shell's app-method hook, PacketShark's `capture.*` methods, Vision's
picture sharing in `pos-vision`, Settings → Companion). **`gatewayd` is not
in the image**, so no phone can connect to a 0.5.0 card; Settings →
Companion says so. Shipping the gateway is waiting on the security items in
issue #172 (docs/services/GATEWAYD.md). The Android app is not published
with this release.

### Fleet over the local network: in source only

Fleet's LAN link profiles, match saves, the `peerd` peer service and the
LAN lobby (PRs #153-#164; ADR-017) are on master and tested end to end on
the host. **`peerd` is not in the image**, so Fleet offers the LAN only
where `peerd` runs; on a 0.5.0 card Fleet plays over the mesh as before.

the root file system checks below confirm the binaries absent.

and no earlier build output, the vendor dependencies at the pins in

## Upgrading from v0.4.0

A flash replaces the whole card: save `/var/lib/pocketos`, `/etc/default`
and `/etc/pocketos` first. A locally installed optional Vision model file
has to be copied again. Shell, `pos-vision` and `pos-capture` are installed
together by the image.

## Known limitations

- **No whole-image hardware test** for 0.5.0 (Validation, above): first
  boot, the kernel, services and the first-boot path of this exact image
  are covered only by the image gate and the comparison with v0.4.0's
  image (Release image).
- **`style_lint` fails** (Release preparation): colour literals in the card
  tables and the Zabbix console. Not a user-visible defect.
- **Companion and Fleet LAN are not usable from the image** (`gatewayd`,
  `peerd` not shipped). The Companion gateway's open items are issue #172;
  issue #171 is a flaky host test of its mesh path.
- **PacketShark**: no monitor mode; Wi-Fi capture sees only the unit's own
  link. The Unit A line-rate gate has not been run; portrait, physical
  touch and keys of M10-M12.1 are unchecked on a unit (PACKETSHARK.md §10).
- **Zabbix**: DS §35 and docs/apps/ZABBIX.md still describe the previous
  layout.
- **RIFT Activity**: no real radio frame has been seen by this build on
  hardware.
- Everything listed for v0.4.0 and earlier still applies unless fixed above
  (docs/releases/v0.4.0.md, docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md), including the
  `calendar_lint`, `terminal_lint` and `term_session_test` items.
