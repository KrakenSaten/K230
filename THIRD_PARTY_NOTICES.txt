Training data of Vision's R0 detector

The R0 detector (/usr/share/doors/vision/det-r0-traffic6-yolox-tiny-416.kmodel,
an experimental beta, licensed by the Doors project under the Apache License
2.0) was trained by the Doors project, from no pretrained weights, on the
traffic6_r0 dataset: images and annotations selected from the two sources
below and filtered to six classes (car, truck, bus, motorcycle,
bicycle, person). No image or annotation from them is in the Doors image.
None of their authors or publishers endorses Doors or this model, and they
give no warranty for it.

    Copyright the COCO Consortium, https://cocodataset.org/
    Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
    licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Changes: filtered to the six classes above and relabelled 1-6.

Open Images Dataset V7 annotations
    Copyright Google LLC, https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/index.html
    Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
    licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Changes: filtered to the six classes above; Car, Limousine, Van and Taxi
    folded into car, and Man, Woman, Boy and Girl into person, following

    Photographs from Flickr, as listed by COCO 2017 and Open Images V7, each

    published as ATTRIBUTION.tsv beside the Doors release that carries the
    model. COCO records no photographer names; its rows name the photo's
    Flickr file instead.
